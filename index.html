Our Car Wash Services

Cave Wave Car Wash is a professional car wash provider serving communities across East Texas. We are committed to delivering the highest quality car wash experience, combining cutting-edge technology with exceptional customer service. Our facilities are designed to handle vehicles of all sizes, from compact cars to full-size trucks and SUVs, ensuring every customer drives away satisfied.

Express Exterior Wash

Our express exterior wash is perfect for customers on the go. In just a few minutes, your vehicle receives a thorough cleaning using our advanced tunnel system. High-pressure pre-soak, foam bath, soft-cloth wash, spot-free rinse, and high-velocity drying — all included in every wash. Our express service is fast, affordable, and delivers results that rival hand washing, without the wait or the hassle.

Fast Pass Unlimited Wash Club

Join the Cave Wave Fast Pass Club for unlimited washes at one low monthly price. Members enjoy exclusive benefits including priority lane access, discounted detailing services, and the freedom to wash as often as they like. Whether you drive through rain, dust, or daily commutes, your vehicle will always look its best. The Fast Pass Club is the smartest way to keep your car clean year-round while saving money on every wash.

Premium Detail Station

For those who want the ultimate clean, our premium detail station offers interior and exterior detailing services. Our trained technicians use professional-grade products and equipment to restore your vehicle's appearance. From hand waxing and clay bar treatments to interior shampooing and leather conditioning, we provide comprehensive detailing that protects your investment and keeps your vehicle looking showroom-new.

Free Vacuum & Mat Cleaning

Every Cave Wave location features complimentary self-service vacuum stations and mat cleaning equipment. After your tunnel wash, take advantage of our powerful vacuums to clean your interior, floor mats, and upholstery. It's just one more way we go above and beyond to deliver a complete car care experience at every visit.
